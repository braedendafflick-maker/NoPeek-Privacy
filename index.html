NoPeek Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

NoPeek does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data. Period.

What NoPeek stores

All data lives exclusively on your device, in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Your settings (theme, distraction list, allowlist, goals, break preferences)
  • Your active focus session (task name, timer, peeks, parked thoughts, checklist)
  • Your local session history (past sessions, durations, peek counts)

What NoPeek does NOT do

  • No data ever leaves your device — there is no server, no sync, no account
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting
  • No reading or transmitting of web page content — the content script only draws the focus screen over sites you chose to hide
  • No cookies, no fingerprinting, no ads, no third-party services

Your control

  • Export your entire history as JSON or CSV from Settings → Stats
  • Delete your history with one click from Settings → Stats
  • Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes all stored data
